Gaslight Tour

Founded in 2010, the Gaslight Tour features local history as theatre. Each year a historic theme tells a particular story of Collingwood.

2026 Theme — If These Walls Could Talk

What secrets lie behind the bricks and mortar of Collingwood’s heritage buildings?

  • WhenOctober 24 – November 1
  • WhereSimcoe Street Theatre, 65 Simcoe Street, Collingwood
  • FormatFour locally written 20-minute plays. Light refreshments at intermission are included in the ticket price.

This year’s plays

  • Hank’s Pranks

    The training of a new server takes a turn for the unexpected when the restaurant’s resident spirit decides to make his presence known at closing time, sparking heated discussion among the believers and non-believers on staff.

    • Playwright: Monica Wilson
    • Director: Shirley Joost
  • These Stone Walls

    Within “These Stone Walls”, we witness Collingwood residents preparing to shelter an escaping family on the Underground Railroad. But as the night unfolds, it becomes clear that each person has arrived at this threshold for different reasons and with different levels of commitment to the cause.

    • Playwright: Mandy St. Germaine
    • Director: Conrad Boyce
  • Ode to the Back Wall of the Curling Club

    In 1916, a young Collingwood man must choose between staying home to create a life with his love or answering the call of King and country to go and fight in the fields of France.

    • Playwright: Dawn Hanley
    • Director: Jane Bolton
  • Ghosts of Elephants Past

    Three writers attempt to create a play about Collingwood’s history, only to discover that the town’s past includes circus elephants, furious neighbours, and more chaos than they bargained for. As imagination collides with heritage, the walls of the Hodgson House reveal stories no one would believe.

    • Playwright: Joanne White
    • Director: Carlo Gallina

About the Tour

The annual Gaslight Tour — now in armchair-tour format — provides entertainment through performances by local people of original plays written by local people. The format may change over time; our plays always showcase the history of Collingwood.
