2022 — Unsolved Mysteries

Four plays by four women playwrights, crafted around Collingwood history and unsolved mysteries.

Photo Credit: Bryan Davies Photography and mycollingwood.ca

The plays

  • Firebug

    It is 1886 and two star-crossed lovers are caught between the destruction of an unknown arsonist and the rivalry between their families.

    • Playwright: Dawn Hanley
  • Glory

    A young girl named Glory Whalen was murdered in Collingwood, a century ago. This mystery remains unsolved today.

    • Playwright: Eleanor Burke
  • The Sorcerer

    As the shipping and sailing season comes to an end in November of 1879, fierce storms howl across Georgian Bay. G.P. Burkitt, captain of the Waubuno, comes into conflict with the steamer’s owners, its passengers, and crew, and his own conscience as he contemplates the ship’s departure from Collingwood Harbour.

    • Playwright: Janet Wilkinson
  • The Collingwood Bomberettes

    World War Two factory workers prepare for an upcoming beauty contest. One contestant is missing.

    • Playwright: Melissa Shaw

2021 — Cemetery Stories

An outdoor return to live performance at First Presbyterian Church Cemetery on Poplar Side Road in Collingwood. The Gaslight Writing Team researched and wrote five ten-minute scenes — some that bring a tear to your eye, others that make you laugh out loud. Gaslight Guides led small tour groups through the cemetery with local folklore, customs, and superstitions, stopping along the way as local actors brought each story to life.

Photo Credit: mycollingwood.ca
