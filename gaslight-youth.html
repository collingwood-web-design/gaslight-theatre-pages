Gaslight Youth

Supporting young people in Collingwood through workshops, drama camp subsidies, and graduation bursaries.

2025 bursaries

The Gaslight Community Theatre Productions Bursary recognizes a local graduating student who has made a significant contribution to theatre or dramatic arts and/or who intends to pursue post-secondary theatre or film studies.

In June 2025, Alyssa Rowbotham (Our Lady of the Bay Catholic High School) and Raine Sammons (Collingwood Collegiate Institute) received GCTP bursaries at their graduation ceremonies.

Alyssa received her bursary from former Gaslight Board director Chelsea Coulter. Raine volunteered with Gaslight before graduating, helping with stage tech.

Bursary program — new in 2024

In June 2024, for the first time, the bursary was presented to Dawn Morby (Our Lady of the Bay) and Mylie Farrell (Collingwood Collegiate Institute).

Gaslight has a long tradition of supporting young people — hosting workshops and providing financial support to local youth drama camps.
