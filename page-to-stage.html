Page to Stage

This is the teaching side of Gaslight Community Theatre Productions — opportunities for local residents to develop skills in scene-writing, playwriting, directing, acting, and stage managing under the guidance of professionals in the field.

  • WhereSimcoe Street Theatre

Course offerings

We subsidize two Page-to-Stage writing programs each year. The Scene-Writing Course offers the expertise of successful professional playwright Dan Needles. The Playwriting Experience is led by Dawn Hanley and Janet Wilkinson — educators and long-time Gaslight playwrights who, between them, have seen 14 plays produced for the Tour. They walk you through research and writing so you can complete a tour-worthy play.

Scene-writing course — Jan to Mar 2026

Each winter we offer a scene-writing course with playwright and author Dan Needles, best known as the creator of the Wingfield Farm series. His plays have appeared in virtually every English-language theatre in Canada. Participants gather to learn how to write a 10-minute scene.

  • WhereThe Duff House
  • WhenWednesdays 6–9 pm · January 28 to March 4, 2026 (5 weeks)
  • Cost$150
  • Contactjanet.gaslight@gmail.com

Once the final scenes are presented to the writing group, several are chosen for rehearsal with volunteer actors. Rehearsals culminate in a staged reading at Simcoe Street Theatre in front of a live audience.

Try acting — staged readings

A wonderful opportunity to try acting for the first time with our staged reading performance, 6 Scenes & A Cup of Tea. Rehearsals are a short commitment — about five hours over three dates — and lead to two performances.

Each scene is roughly 10 minutes. No experience necessary. Our director and stage manager guide you through it all. It’s a fun way to try something new and meet people in the community.

Contact janet.gaslight@gmail.com.

The Playwriting Experience — 2026

Interested in learning how to write a 20-minute play? Janet Wilkinson and Dawn Hanley guide beginner and experienced playwrights through setting a scene, writing dialogue, and creating drama. This is a collective writing experience — four plays are selected and brought to life for the Fall 2026 Gaslight Tour, If These Walls Could Talk.

  • WhereCommunity room B/C, third floor, Collingwood Public Library, 55 Ste Marie St.
  • WhenSpring 2026 · Saturdays 12:00–4:00 pm
  • Cost$100 per person
  • Registerdawn.gaslight@gmail.com

Dawn and Janet are long-time Gaslight playwrights; between them they have seen 14 plays produced for the Tour. Writing programs fill quickly — don’t miss your chance to join.
