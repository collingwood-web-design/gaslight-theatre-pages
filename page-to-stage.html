Course offerings

We subsidize two Page-to-Stage writing programs each year. The Scene-Writing Course offers the expertise of playwright Dan Needles. The Playwriting Experience is led by Dawn Hanley and Janet Wilkinson — educators and long-time Gaslight playwrights who, between them, have seen 14 plays produced for the Tour.

Scene-writing course — Jan to Mar 2026

Each winter, participants gather with playwright and author Dan Needles — creator of the Wingfield Farm series — to learn how to write a 10-minute scene.

  • WhereThe Duff House
  • WhenWednesdays 6–9 pm · January 28 to March 4, 2026 (5 weeks)
  • Cost$150
  • Contactjanet.gaslight@gmail.com

Four scenes move on to rehearsal with volunteer actors, culminating in a staged reading at Simcoe Street Theatre.

The Playwriting Experience

Janet Wilkinson and Dawn Hanley guide beginner and experienced playwrights through setting a scene, writing dialogue, and creating drama. Four plays are brought to life for that year’s Gaslight Tour.

To register, email dawn.gaslight@gmail.com.
