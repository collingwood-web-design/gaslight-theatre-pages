Who we are

About Us

Gaslight Community Theatre Productions is a not-for-profit amateur theatre enterprise that got its start in 2010 with The Gaslight Tour.

What we do

Funds generated through ticket sales, donations, and sponsorships are used in ways that benefit our whole community.

The Gaslight Tour

The annual Gaslight Tour — now in armchair-tour format — provides entertainment through performances by local people of original plays written by local people. Our plays always showcase the history of Collingwood. We sell out quickly every year.

Page-to-Stage writing programs

We subsidize two writing programs each year for adults in our community. Small class sizes, low tuition, and personal attention make these programs a hit. Janet Wilkinson produces the Scene Writing Course taught by Dan Needles. Dawn Hanley produces The Playwriting Experience.

Gaslight Youth

Since 2017 we have subsidized tuition for children to attend drama camps. In 2024 we started a bursary for graduating students from both Collingwood secondary schools who plan to pursue studies in theatre or film.

Vision statement

Gaslight Community Theatre Productions seeks to provide an outlet for creative inclusive community involvement while producing quality education and entertainment in a safe and supportive space.

Land acknowledgement

It is our privilege to come together on the traditional territory of the Anishinaabeg. Knowledge of our shared past compels us to confront the long history of colonization and its dire impact on Indigenous peoples, and we are further committed to repairing rifts and building meaningful partnerships as we move together into the future. We are honoured to share stories in the tradition of the Indigenous peoples who, for thousands of years, walked upon and cared for the lands we all now call home.

What we did

Over a decade ago, Arlene Noble brought the idea of the Gaslight Tour to Collingwood after attending a similar theatre experience in Waterford, Ontario. With support from the Collingwood Rotary Club and a great team of volunteers, the Tour was born. The Collingwood community embraced the project from the start.
